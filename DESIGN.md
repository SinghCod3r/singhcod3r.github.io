# Design

## World

An editorial systems portfolio: warm paper, ink-black type, acid chartreuse, and electric lilac refracted through frosted glass. It should feel like a well-made independent publication that happens to move like a live system.

## Typography

- UI/body: Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Display: Syne.
- Editorial emphasis: Instrument Serif italic.
- Display type is oversized but capped so the page remains legible at every width.

## Surfaces

- Main canvas: near-black ink with soft aurora color fields.
- Glass: `backdrop-filter: blur(30px) saturate(190%)`, translucent white, 1px specular border.
- Depth comes from soft offset shadows and perspective, not flat neon outlines.

## Motion

- One continuous authored thread: a cursor-reactive aurora field, scroll-linked reveals, and a velocity-aware ribbon.
- Entering content uses transform/opacity with strong ease-out and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- Hover tilt is pointer-gated and never required to understand content.

## Signature

The wordmark "SinghCod3r" appears as a massive footer instrument. OpenPrinting is represented as a quiet live thread rather than as an extra badge.
